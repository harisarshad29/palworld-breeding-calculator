    How Palworld Breeding Works

    Breeding in Palworld uses a combination system where specific parent pairs can produce special children, while regular pairs follow breeding-power rules. This calculator helps you test both approaches quickly and compare outcomes.

    What You Can Do

    • Calculate child results from two selected parents.
    • Find all parent combinations for a chosen target Pal.
    • Check map locations, item sources, and technology milestones.
    • Compare a breeding-power difficulty heuristic, not an in-game capture probability.

    People Also Ask

    How do I breed Anubis? Use the reverse calculator and fastest Anubis breed guide to compare parent pairs.

    How do I find best breeding combos? Start with the Reverse Calculator, select your target Pal, and compare all listed parent pairs and method types.

    Can legendary pals breed? Many legendary species require two parents of their own species. For example, Jetragon + Jetragon produces Jetragon; reverse lookup shows supported pairs.

    Does gender matter? Breeding requires a male and female parent. Katress + Wixen has different outcomes depending on which parent is male; set both genders for this pair.

    Why is breeding not working? See our breeding troubleshooting guide — check Breeding Farm setup, cake in its chest, suitable male and female parents, and valid pairs.

    Best early game breeding combo? See our early game combo guide for easy parent routes.

    Fastest flying mount breeding? Use the Jetragon breeding guide and reverse lookup.

    Can I use this on mobile? Yes. The interface is responsive for quick breeding checks on the go.
