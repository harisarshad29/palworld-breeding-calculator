How to Breed Astegon in Palworld

Breeding power: 490. 104 parent routes can produce Astegon.

Follow this route: unlock breeding → pick parents from the table → run eggs in the incubator. Use the breeding calculator with Astegon pre-selected as target.

Quick steps

  1. Build Breeding Farm + Egg Incubator and keep cake stocked.
  2. Open the all combos for Astegon page or table below.
  3. Choose parents already available in your save. Breeding power is not a capture probability.
  4. Place parents, hatch the child, repeat if you need better passives.
