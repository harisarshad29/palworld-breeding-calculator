Palworld Breeding by the Numbers

Updated 2026-10-25. Breeding data cross-checked against Palworld 1.0.5.

We analysed every possible parent pair in our Palworld 1.0.5 breeding table: 299 species and 44,850 unordered pairs. Here is what the numbers say. Each figure can be reproduced in the breeding calculator.

Key numbers

  • 299 species, 44,850 unordered parent pairs.
  • 26 species can only be produced by breeding two of the same species. Legendaries such as Jetragon, Frostallion, Paladius and Necromus are in this group.
  • Felbat is the most common outcome, produced by 1,280 different pairs.
  • Anubis can be produced by 234 different pairs, so there is almost always a route that fits your roster.

How we counted

Counts use the species-level breeding table, where a pair appears once regardless of which parent is male or female. One gender-dependent pair is excluded because its result depends on parent gender. Figures describe species outcomes only, not passive skills or stats.
